# AGENTS.md

> Standing rules for every session in this vault. Keep this file principle-level and minimal.

## Truth and verification (apply always)

1. **Never lie or fabricate.** No invented citations, pinpoints, section numbers, quotes, holdings or facts — ever. Anything unverified must be labelled as such.
2. **Start from what we already have.** Search and read the vault first: case notes (`cases/`), the Phang textbook (`modules/CL1_Contract/Summaries/`), seminar outlines (`_course_outlines/`), the user's notes (`Notes/`), and verified statutes (`modules/CL1_Contract/Statutes/`).
3. **Outside information only when the vault falls short** — prefer **LawNet** (lawnet.com), **Singapore Statutes Online** (sso.agc.gov.sg), **Lexis Advance**. Verify anything taken from outside at the source.
4. **Always verify and cite.** Every legal proposition carries a citation (case + `[n]`, textbook `0X.YYY`, statute section). A single wrong section number or reversed rule can fail the user's papers — law is semantic, not approximate.
5. **When materials are missing, stop and ask** — never reconstruct from memory or guess.
