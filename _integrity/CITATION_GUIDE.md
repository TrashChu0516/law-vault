# Citation Guide — Singapore Law

> **The rule is simple: your reader must be able to find, with zero guesswork, the exact page or paragraph where every proposition comes from.** If they cannot, the citation is incomplete.

---

## 1. Cases (Singapore)

**Neutral citation first (if available), then law report:**

```
Chwee Kin Keong v Digilandmall.com Pte Ltd [2005] 1 SLR(R) 502 (CA) at [91]
```

The parts:
- **Party names** (italicised in print; in plain Markdown, leave plain or use `*...*`)
- **Year** in square brackets `[2005]` (or round `(1871)` for older cases where the year is part of the volume)
- **Volume + report series**: `1 SLR(R)` = Singapore Law Reports (Reissue)
- **Page**: `502`
- **Court** in parentheses: `(CA)` Court of Appeal, `(HC)` High Court
- **Pinpoint**: `at [91]` (paragraph) or `at 607` (page) — **never omit the pinpoint**

**Common Singapore report series:**
| Abbrev. | Full name |
|---------|-----------|
| `SLR` / `SLR(R)` | Singapore Law Reports / (Reissue) |
| `SGCA` | Singapore Court of Appeal (neutral citation) |
| `SGHC` | Singapore High Court (neutral citation) |

> ⚠️ Neutral citations are authoritative and preferred: `[2025] SGCA 20` beats `[2025] 2 SLR 100` where available. Note the outline used `Chwee Kin Keong [2004] 2 SLR(R) 594` (the **HC** decision) — do not confuse it with the CA decision at `[2005] 1 SLR(R) 502`. **Verify the court, not just the name.**

## 2. Cases (UK / Commonwealth)

```
Carlill v Carbolic Smoke Ball Co [1893] 1 QB 256 (CA) 261
Smith v Hughes (1871) LR 6 QB 597, 607
```
- `[1893]` = square brackets when year identifies the volume; `(1871)` = round brackets when it is a volume number.
- Common series: `QB`, `KB`, `AC`, `WLR`, `All ER`. Decode any abbreviation via the **Cardiff Index**.

## 3. Statutes (Singapore)

```
Unfair Contract Terms Act (Cap 396, 1994 Rev Ed) s 2(1)
```
- **Cap** = Chapter number in the Singapore Statutes
- **Rev Ed** = Revised Edition year
- Pinpoint to **section and subsection**: `s 2(1)`, `ss 12–15`

## 4. Legislation (UK)

```
Sale of Goods Act 1979 (UK), s 14(2)
```

## 5. Journal Articles

```
Author, "Title" (Year) Volume Journal Abbrev StartPage, PinpointPage
Bryan Leow, "Noting Up in Singapore" (2025) 37 Sing Ac LJ 100, 105
```

## 6. Books / Textbooks

```
Andrew Phang (gen ed), The Law of Contract in Singapore (2nd ed, Academy Publishing, 2022) at para 03.001
```
- Always cite the **edition** — the 1st ed (2012) and 2nd ed (2022) differ materially.

## 7. The Four-Tier Citation Discipline (from the Anti-Plagiarism Guide)

| Tier | What | Requirement |
|------|------|-------------|
| 1 | **Direct quote** | Quotation marks + pinpoint |
| 2 | **Paraphrase of a legal principle** | Still cite the authority (the idea is not yours) |
| 3 | **Assertion about the law** | Always supported by authority |
| 4 | **Your own analysis** | Marked as yours ("It is submitted…") |

> **Golden rule**: if the sentence contains a proposition of law and has no citation, either (a) add the citation, or (b) it is wrong. There is no third option.

---

## 8. Common Citation Errors to Avoid

| Error | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Citing `Chwee Kin Keong [2004]` when you mean the CA `[2005]` decision | Always confirm the court |
| Citing a case without a pinpoint | Add `at [para]` or `at page` |
| Citing a secondary source as authority | Trace to the primary source |
| Using the 1st edition page number for the 2nd edition | Confirm the edition |
| Forgetting the note-up status | Add "still good law as at [date]" |
